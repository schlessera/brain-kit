A turn on the egress proxy treats mail submission as outward by default, next to the GitHub API: a connection to port 25, 465 or 587 on any host, or to a built-in mail-send API such as `gmail.googleapis.com` or `api.sendgrid.com`, waits on the turn's approval card unless the call in progress was classified as a read or an approved outward action to that host. `outward.hosts` still adds the rest. Microsoft Graph and Exchange Online carry more than mail, so their reads by an opaque script also ask.
