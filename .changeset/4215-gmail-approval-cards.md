New `GoogleMailApprovalCard`, the approval card for one Gmail write (#4215): send, draft save, draft delete, trash and modify. It takes props and callbacks only and is built on `ApprovalCard`. A send shows the exact envelope: From, each recipient with its send-list match, Bcc on its own emphasised row, Reply-To, the subject and what it replies to. The envelope summary counts Bcc. An empty Cc, Bcc or Reply-To row is omitted. The whole body sits in a keyboard-scrollable region with a "↓ n more lines" cue that counts the lines not yet wholly in view, and Allow is never gated on scrolling. The body is drawn exactly as it will be sent: one numbered row per line, every character as written, blank lines, indentation, runs of spaces and tabs kept, long lines wrapped, and hidden characters, a lone carriage return included, shown as markers. It is never read as Markdown and holds no links. Draft save shows the same envelope and body. An update shows its body change, line by line as the full body draws it, when the host passes the previous body (`previousBody`; today's payload has none). When the host passes the draft's message (today's payload carries only its id), draft delete shows its envelope and the body clamped to six rows behind "Show whole draft". Trash and modify list the affected messages, five then "+N more". Modify states each label change in words. Irreversible cards (send, draft delete, trash, and any modify or draft save the host classed irreversible) show no standing choice and have no `a` key. A reversible card shows the one standing choice its chat allows, with a note. A tainted chat puts its banner above the payload. Addresses, display names, the subject, the In-Reply-To ids and the affected messages' senders and subjects are drawn through `Field`, with hidden characters shown as markers; the send-as address in the head shows its markers too. Gmail's message and draft ids, which the host validates, are drawn as plain text. `googleApprovalKeys` gives the single keys a card answers to, for printing and binding alike. `GOOGLE_SEND_RECIPIENT_REFUSAL` is the refusal sentence for a send the host refused. `ApprovalCard` gains optional `top`, `alwaysLabel` and `alwaysNote` props, and `shortcuts.allow` becomes optional. Existing cards render as before. The icon map adds `mail`, `trash` and `hidden-copy`.
