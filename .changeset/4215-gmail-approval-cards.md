New `GoogleMailApprovalCard`, the approval card for one Gmail write (#4215): send, draft save, draft delete, trash and modify. It takes props and callbacks only and is built on `ApprovalCard`. A send shows the exact envelope: From, each recipient with its send-list match, Bcc on its own emphasised row, Reply-To, the subject and what it replies to. The envelope summary counts Bcc. An empty Cc, Bcc or Reply-To row is omitted. The whole body sits in a keyboard-scrollable region with a "↓ n more lines" cue, and Allow is never gated on scrolling. Draft save shows the same envelope; an update shows its body change. Draft delete clamps the draft behind "Show whole draft". Trash and modify list the affected messages, five then "+N more". Modify states each label change in words. Irreversible cards (send, draft delete, trash, and any modify or draft save the host classed irreversible) show no standing choice and have no `a` key. A reversible card shows the one standing choice its chat allows, with a note. A tainted chat puts its banner above the payload. Every address, display name, subject, message id and body line is drawn through `Field` or `UntrustedText`, with hidden characters shown as markers and links behind the interstitial. `googleApprovalKeys` gives the single keys a card answers to, for printing and binding alike. `GOOGLE_SEND_RECIPIENT_REFUSAL` is the refusal sentence for a send the host refused. `ApprovalCard` gains optional `top`, `alwaysLabel` and `alwaysNote` props, and `shortcuts.allow` becomes optional. Existing cards render as before. The icon map adds `mail`, `trash` and `hidden-copy`.
