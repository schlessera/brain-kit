Live conversation now draws its state in the tall voice field from `@schlessera/brain-ui-kit` (#3699). The field's colour, meter, icon and one word say who has the floor (Connecting, Listening, Thinking, Working, Speaking, Needs your tap, Muted, Reconnecting), with the service and the connected time beside it, and Mute, Interrupt and End sit on its raised controls. Interrupt is enabled only while a reply plays. A failure that withdraws capture shows "Live conversation stopped" with the client's reason, and Start again reopens the disclosure. Nothing stacks above the field any more: its body shows one message at a time (a held draft, then the tap-only refusal line, then reply audio off, then the client's notice), a held draft reads "Draft · not sent yet" with Send now and Discard draft in the controls row (taking Interrupt's place unless brain is speaking), Recover reply audio stays in the row while reply audio is off, and waiting for voice readiness reads "mic off". Incomplete startup-context coverage is an inline notice in the chat at the start of the live section, and background tasks leave the field for the chat. A blocked microphone now says to allow it, then Start again. The two diagnostic status lines ("● Listening · mic on", "Connected · chat: ok"), the provider-code details button and the Mute mic, Stop reply and Leave voice buttons are gone. The conversation client exposes its capture intent (`capture`: `idle`, `listening`, `muted` or `stopped`) and display-only meter readings (`levels()`); neither changes capture, recovery or permission behaviour.
