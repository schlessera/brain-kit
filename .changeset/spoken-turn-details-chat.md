A spoken turn's transcript details now open from the turn itself (#3700). The glyph button under each recognized or voice-reply turn is gone. Each turn has one hidden "Details" control that shows as a pill when you Tab to it, or after you hover the turn. A right-click, Shift+F10, the ContextMenu key or a touch long-press opens the same dialog. A right-click while you have text selected in the turn keeps the browser's own menu, so Copy still works. The details themselves and their wording are unchanged.
