A turn that began with a permanent safety recording gap no longer refuses its ordinary network traffic, or stops, when the writer tolerates a lost record (#4351). On such a turn the occurrence writer returns `{id:0, recorded:false}` by design, because the gap already shows the loss. An untrusted proxy connection now proceeds under the restricted recipient policy it already passed, without source trust, instead of refusing with 503 `egress_unavailable`. A lost terminal exposure no longer stops the turn. Such a turn raises no approval card and uses none it holds: a connection that would need a Network card (an ordinary destination or a managed GitHub fetch) or an outward card is refused without one, recorded with reason `recording_gap` and answered with a 403 naming it. A card pending or answered when the gap appears is never used. Allow-listed and already-contacted destinations still connect, and a new turn restores approvals. The SDK egress proxy takes the gap from a new `recordingGap()` option. Source-trusted connections still need a durable occurrence and refuse with 503 without one, and every other lost write, including a refused write such as a full recording queue, a lost connection audit or any loss on a turn without a permanent gap, keeps its existing containment.
