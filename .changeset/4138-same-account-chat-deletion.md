A chat two owner logins used can be deleted by either of them (#4138). Every owner sign-in is its own principal, and explicit chat deletion compared exact principals across the chat's ownership records, so a chat continued by voice from a second device was refused to both logins as `unavailable`. `POST /api/chat-deletions` now admits any usable owner login when every recorded owner (the dialogue owner, each voice epoch, the typed-creation binding, a provisional chat's safety collections) proves that same owner account, also after another login has been pruned. Migration 091 stores the durable account beside each voice epoch and typed-creation binding, from the principal's own row, and backfills legacy rows only where a principal row still proves it. A record of another account, a pruned login with no stored account, or corrupt or contradictory evidence still refuses that chat alone. The caller is checked from its own current row at admission and at commit; revoked, expired, agent, ambient and system callers stay `unauthorized`. The operation, its receipt and its retry key stay with the login that started it, original attribution is never rewritten, and live conversation and epoch control, busy refusal, stale review, permanent fences and per-store outcomes are unchanged. No request, receipt or refusal shape changes.
