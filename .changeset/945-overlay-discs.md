The kit gains `DiscButton` and `DiscRow`, the overlay disc: a 32px paint in a 44px button with `ink` or `mute` rest ink and an optional label that expands leftward on hover and keyboard focus, instantly under reduced motion. `SuggestionItem` gains optional `cost`, printed as a gold effect chip, and `disabled` with `why`, printed at rest; a disabled chip stays focusable and ignores clicks, taps, Enter and Space. Interactive chips are at least 44px tall under a coarse pointer. Icons gain `latest`. The Chat page's New chat and scroll discs now use `DiscButton`: New chat is ink at rest and opens to its label, and the scroll disc is named `Scroll to latest` with a 44px target.
