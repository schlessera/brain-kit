A tainted Network approval card now offers four choices (#3595): **Allow once**, **Allow this origin for this session**, **Allow all origins for this session** and **Deny**.

- **Wire (ui-sdk).** `ServerToolApprovalRequest.network` (`NetworkApprovalOffer`: `transport` `http` or `connect`, `host`, effective `port` and the offered `scopes`) advertises the destination's origin. `ClientToolApproval.networkScope` (`NetworkApprovalScope`: `once`, `origin` or `session`) answers it, and `ServerToolResolution.networkScope` reports the granted scope. `PermissionRequest.network` and the allow `PermissionDecision.networkScope` carry them to and from backends. Every field is optional: a reply without a scope keeps the earlier host-wide session answer, and a scope the card did not offer narrows to once.
- **Host (ui-server).** Once allows one connection and asks again, also for a concurrent connection to the same destination and in later turns. An origin grant covers the same transport, host and port, so HTTP and an opaque CONNECT tunnel stay distinct. A session grant covers every destination. Each is two new `network_grant` governance events for the chat session, a `proposed` one committed through the governance writer and a `settled` one written as the card settles; neither grants anything alone. A grant holds across later turns and reconnects of the same session only. A connection admitted under a scope records it as `networkScope` on its `egress_connection` and never becomes a contacted host. The settlement happens after the card revalidates and before the host publishes the granted outcome; a grant that cannot be proposed or settled, or whose connection has ended, expires the card. With the write worker the settled record commits just after the outcome is published; if it fails, the turn stops and later turns ask again. A wider grant covers only connections that start after it. Each managed GitHub acquisition gets its own card, which offers no scope and covers that one fetch, and no grant covers one. Voice turns never use these grants. Deny and allow lists, byte limits, outward approvals, voice and autonomous turns are otherwise unchanged.
- **Cards (ui-react, ui-kit).** The transcript card and the Actions card show the four labels; Allow, and the `a` key, are the narrowest choice. Receipts name a scope only once the host's granted outcome arrives. The kit `ApprovalCard` gains `widerAllows` for the extra full-width choices.
