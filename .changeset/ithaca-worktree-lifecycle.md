Add the host lifecycle for managed reference worktrees. A worktree is allocated from authorized shared source as a writable snapshot at a fixed reported commit, belongs to one principal and is granted only to an open worker generation of that principal after its authority, including a live grant for private source, is checked again. Only explicit access, resumption or renewal resets its three-day inactivity clock; a live worker keeps it past expiry, a provably ended holder is recovered and unknown liveness keeps ownership and charge. Release and expiry record durable cleanup eligibility facts and never delete anything. Workers cannot reach a worktree until journal coverage is wired.
