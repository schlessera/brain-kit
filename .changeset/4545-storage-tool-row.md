New `ToolRow` and `ToolList` for storage maintenance. A row shows what the tool does, its last run as a glyph, word and time (never run, waiting, running, cancelling, done, partly done, refused, cancelled, outcome unknown, or not available on this host), the measured change as `Available +1.8 GB · unattributed`, and a quiet Run, Try again or Cancel button beside the `maintain` effect chip. A missing before or after reads `Change not measured · {reason}`, never `unknown → unknown`. The headroom meter draws available space with a marker at the pack estimate and hatches any shortfall (`▲ Short by …`). Run stays enabled when short, because the server's refusal decides. Without an estimate or a comparable observation, the row shows a `?` in words and draws no bar. Running shows an indeterminate stripe with `Working…`, and no count. The row stacks below 720px of its own width. The list adds the section heading, one footnote pointing to Find large files, and a polite live region. New types: `ToolRowProps`, `ToolListProps`, `ToolRunState`, `ToolChange`, `ToolNotMeasuredReason`, `ToolOutcomeCounts`, `ToolHeadroom` and `ToolHeadroomFigure`.
