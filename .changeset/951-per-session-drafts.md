Every session keeps its own composer draft. Text and images typed in one session stay with it: switching sessions, opening Actions, Files or Settings, and remounting the composer bring back exactly that session's draft, and nothing typed in one session appears in another. New chat, from the overlay disc, Sessions `New conversation` and the palette, opens an empty composer and keeps the old draft where it was, with no confirmation. A draft that has no session yet is listed in Sessions under `Drafts` (`Draft with 2 images` when it has no text) and opens again in an empty chat; a session with a draft prints `draft · 2h` on its row. Repeated empty New chats list nothing.

On a host that advertises `capabilities.sessionDrafts`, drafts are saved there and restored after a reload and on the operator's other devices. The line under the composer says what happened: `draft · saved` only once the host acknowledged that exact revision, images included; `draft · saving…`, `draft · not saved yet` while unreachable, `too large to save` and `drafts saved · delete one to save this` for the host's limits, and `draft · this host doesn't keep drafts · kept on this device` without the capability. A draft changed on another device while edited here prints `draft changed on another device · Compare`; `Compare drafts` shows both versions and offers `Keep this device's`, `Keep other's` and `Keep both`, which turns the other version into a Draft entry. Nothing is merged and nothing visible is overwritten.

A send is now a snapshot apart from the draft: the field empties when you send, the message names the saved revision so the host consumes exactly that one, and words typed after sending are the session's next draft. A refused message comes back into the draft. A message the host never confirmed (the connection dropped, or the host refused a frame without naming it) is held where it was sent as a `Didn't hear back` block with `Check again`, `Send again` and `Edit`; nothing is resent on its own. A new conversation's first message that is answered after you have moved on attaches to its own session without taking over the view.
