Adds `brain google` (#4208): `status`, `connect` and the Gmail reads `gmail search`, `gmail read thread|message|draft` and `gmail labels`, each printing `{ ok, operation, data }` or `{ ok: false, operation, error: { code, reason, message } }` with `--json` and exiting 0 or 1. The reads run the same Google operations, decision function, gog transport and projections as the hosted tools, under the governance policy's `google` block in the standalone posture: no session, so no taint, no remembered approval and no card, a `--view` of `content` (the default, third-party text under `untrusted`) or `structured`, `hidden_labels` enforced on every result (a hidden thread reads as a miss), and host cursors kept in the Google home for paging. `status` reports the home, the pinned gog, the policy, the connected alias and the scopes gog recorded for it, warns on a grant broader than the policy can use (`gmail.modify` only while it can admit a write), and says plainly that a local agent with shell access is inside the standalone boundary. `connect <address>` runs gog's OAuth flow into the home with `--services` from the policy, read-only scopes unless `--write`, and with `--write` exactly `gmail.readonly` and `gmail.modify`; `--remote` splits it into a printed URL and an `--auth-url` exchange, `--credentials FILE` stores the OAuth client first, and a recorded grant broader than asked for is removed and refused; a successful connect forgets every search cursor. The home is `BRAIN_GOOGLE_HOME` (default `$XDG_DATA_HOME/brain/google`) with `BRAIN_GOOGLE_KEYRING_PASSWORD` and `BRAIN_GOOGLE_GOG_PATH`, or in an operator's shell on a host `BRAIN_UI_GOOGLE_HOME` with `BRAIN_UI_GOOGLE_KEYRING_PASSWORD` and `BRAIN_UI_GOG_PATH`; a home inside or holding a brain repository, or below a directory another user can change, refuses `connect` and every read, and `brain doctor` reports it in a `google-home` check.
