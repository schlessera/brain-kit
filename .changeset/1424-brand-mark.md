Ship the brain-kit logo. `BrandMark` renders the mark, or with `variant="lockup"` the mark beside the outlined `brain-kit` wordmark, in the theme's ink and amber tokens; below 24px it switches to the small-size master, and it is decorative unless given a `label`. The fourteen master SVGs ship in the package and resolve as `@schlessera/brain-ui-kit/brand/<file>`; the new React-free `@schlessera/brain-ui-kit/brand` entry lists them in `BRAND_MASTERS`.
