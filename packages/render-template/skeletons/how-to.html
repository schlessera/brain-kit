Build a raft that will hold

Twenty trees, four days and a bronze axe. Built as wide as a merchant ship, it rides out weather a narrow hull would not.

Build
4 days
Trees
20
Hands
2

You need

  • A double-edged bronze axe
  • A polished adze
  • Augers
  • Twenty trees: alder, poplar, fir
  • Pegs and dowels
  • Willow withies
  • Sailcloth
  • Braces and sheets
  • Rollers to launch on

Ask for the tools first

The axe, the adze and the sailcloth come from the house. Ask on the first morning, not the fourth.

Method

  1. Fell dry timber. Take trees long dead and seasoned, because they float highest. Fell twenty and trim them with the axe.

  2. Square the timbers. Adze each trunk smooth and true to a line.

  3. Bore and fit. Drill every timber with the augers, then peg and dowel them edge to edge.

  4. Deck and bulwarks. Lay the deck on close-set ribs, as wide as a merchant ship's floor, and fence the sides with woven willow against the sea.

  5. Rig and launch. Step the mast, fit the yard and the steering oar, bend on the sail, and lever her down to the sea on rollers.
