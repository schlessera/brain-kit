The day

    Load the stores: water, bread, one skin of wine.

The route

  1. Off the beach at Ogygia on the ebb.
  2. Stand out past the headland until the island drops astern.
  3. Keep the Great Bear on the left hand, day and night.
  4. Read the hour from the Pleiades and late-setting Boötes.
  5. The mountains of Scheria on the eighteenth day.
Do not correct the bearing at nightA star you cannot see clearly is worse than no star. Hold the course and check it at dawn.

Stow

  • One skin of water
