Brief 1 · 12 July
Delivered as Mentes, a guest-friend

Sail to Pylos and Sparta before the suitors act

For
Telemachus
From
Athena, as Mentes
Decision by
Tomorrow's assembly
Status
Awaiting a ship

Call the assembly, borrow a ship of twenty oars, and ask Nestor and Menelaus what they know. If your father is alive, hold the house one more year. If he is dead, raise a mound and settle your mother's marriage yourself.

01Why now

A hundred and eight suitors have eaten in the hall for four years. Half the stores are gone and the wine will not last another. Nobody in Ithaca has asked Pylos or Sparta for news, and both kings came home from Troy.

02What we give up

The house goes unwatched for a month, with the suitors inside it. Antinous will read the voyage as a challenge, and a ship can be waited for on the way home.

Tell your mother nothingNot until the twelfth day, or until she asks. She will try to stop the ship.

03Next steps

  • Sail at dusk, from the far end of the harbour
