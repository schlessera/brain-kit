# Notes on the crew ledger

Kept since Troy: six hundred men on twelve ships, and the count as it stands after Thrinacia. The [voyage log](https://example.com/voyage-log) has the detail of each loss.

## What the ledger shows

1. **No loss came without a warning.** A seer, a god or the ship's own lookout named every one of them in advance.
2. The two worst days were in harbour, not at sea.
3. The ledger closed on day 1,095, with one survivor.
