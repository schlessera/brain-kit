# Quadrant

Position conceptual priorities with `positional.quadrant`: one-word negative/positive x/y axis tips and one normalized non-axis point per semantic node. Coordinates are honest signed positions in [-1,1]; the builder never clamps an item to another quadrant. Labels remain clear of axes and point strokes. Prefer at most 12 items, with the universal 9-node budget also applying.

[Odysseus example](examples/positional/quadrant.diagram.yaml), pinned to `2026-07-12`.

Adapted from diagram-design commit `b46c9701f035844a20b13ab0551315977188933b` under the module MIT notice. Brain-kit keeps its fonts, solid token paints, 9px floor and white print ground. The source spec remains authoritative; missing authored narrow placement selects the caption/summary and Open diagram viewer, never automatic reflow.
