## The scope

6-inch Dobsonian reflector. Case dimensions: 30 × 12 × 12 in (measured for the
bookshelf bay — see the [[plan]]).

## Collimation (the part I keep re-learning)

1. Center the secondary under the focuser.
2. Align the secondary tilt to the primary.
3. Adjust the primary to center the reflection.
