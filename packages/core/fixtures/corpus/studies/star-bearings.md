## The guide

Calypso's star guide for the seventeen-day passage. Case dimensions: 30 × 12 ×
12 in, allowed for in the raft [[plan]].

## Orientation (the part I keep re-learning)

1. Find the Bear, which does not sink below the horizon.
2. Keep it on the left while sailing from Ogygia.
3. Check the Pleiades and Orion as the night changes.

## Course notes

The navigation course's orientation exercise is the practical version of the
lecture chapter. Night-sailing stars are listed in [[star-catalog]].
