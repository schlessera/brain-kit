## Design

Five-shelf freestanding unit, walnut face frame over a cedar carcass. Through-dovetails
at the top corners, dadoes for the fixed shelves, one drawer at the base.

## Cut list

- 2 × sides — walnut, 72 × 11 in
- 5 × shelves — cedar, 34 × 10 in
- 1 × back panel — cedar shiplap

## Notes

Measured a shelf bay to fit the telescope case when it's not in use — see
[[telescope-setup]] for the case dimensions. The build [[overview]] tracks current
state; sourcing leftovers rolled into the trail-signage cedar order.
