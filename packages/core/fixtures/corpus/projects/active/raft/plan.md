## Design

Twenty trees, squared and joined with pegs and lashings. A pine deck frame,
bulwarks, ballast and a mast. Calypso's adze and auger are needed before
construction can begin; the cloth will be cut when departure is permitted.

## Cut list

- Deck frame: pine, laid across the joined trunks
- Mast and yard: straight stock reserved for the sail
- Lashings: keep enough rope after securing the water cask
